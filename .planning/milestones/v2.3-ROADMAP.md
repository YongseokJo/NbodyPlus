## Milestone Summary

**Key Findings:**
- IrregularForce dominates: 66% of wall time
- MPI overhead significant: 28% of time in message passing
- Load balance excellent: ratio of 1.009
- Primary bottleneck: Dispatch starvation from 10.3M MPI messages/interval

**Optimization Recommendations:**
- Phase 24 (MPI Batching): PROCEED — expected 16-27% speedup
- Phase 25 (Pipelining): CONDITIONAL — 2-5% alone, synergistic with 24

**Deferred Work (to v2.4):**
- Phase 24: MPI Message Batching
- Phase 25: Dispatch Pipelining
- Phase 26: Verification & Benchmarking

**Key Decisions:**
- Rescoped v2.3 from 6 phases to 3 (analysis complete, optimization deferred)
- MPI batching confirmed as best optimization path (incremental, handles CM particles)
- Static partitioning rejected — batching preserves dynamic load balance
