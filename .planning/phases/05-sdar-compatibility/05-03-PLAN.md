## Objective

Add SoA sync calls to `fb_termination.cpp` to ensure particle data consistency when terminating FewBody groups. After SDAR group termination, sync the freed member particles back to SoA.

## Context

In `FBTermination()`:
1. Member particles are extracted from the group
2. Their properties are restored (position, velocity, accelerations, timing)
3. They become active again as regular particles

With SoA as the source of truth, we need to sync the modified member particles back to `particle_data` after termination.
